# Role walk, 2026-10-20: 117 opportunities across every role

Five walkers used the running app as each person who uses it (HR, people ops, recruiter; supervisor, associate, driver; workforce, manager, ops; transport director and marketing; finance, payroll, executive), looking for click savers, workflow gaps and pages that need a UI/UX upgrade to the Fortune 500 standard. Their reports are kept here verbatim so the list survives the session that produced it.

Status (2026-10-21):
- Built and committed: the marketing, communications and settings items (transport/marketing walk 14–23); the kiosk and messages items (floor walk 4, 12–15, and the SOP expiry from 3); the supervisor, ops board and manager items (floor walk 6, 21, 22, the banner date from 3; ops walk 13, 16, 17, 18, 22, 23).
- Not yet built: everything else — payroll, finance and books (finance walk 1–20); time, approvals and scheduling; the transport director and driver items; HR, recruiting and the cross-cutting items.
- Screenshot paths in the reports point at a scratchpad that no longer exists; the findings stand on their own.

## Walk as HR, people ops, recruiter

I walked the three personas in the browser and found 24 problems worth fixing, ranked below. Three of the top four are plain bugs where the app sends people to the wrong place or the wrong work.

Two limits on the walk. The dev data is thin: there are no candidates or offers anywhere and nobody counts as Active on People. So the recruiter's candidate, interview and offer steps (items 8–10) come from reading the code, and the click counts there are estimates. The API also restarted often during the walk. That's how I hit item 24: when the session check fails with a server error, the app shows the login screen. Nothing was submitted, nothing was written to alto_dev, and no repo files were touched.

**2. [bug] Everyone: any brief server error on load signs the user out.**
- Where: `lib/auth.tsx:206-232`. Only a network error or timeout keeps the session. A 500, 502 or 503 falls to the else branch, `setUser(null)`, which goes to `/login`.
